  the answer is bad for the cross-corpus comparison: moving every
  arguable verb takes the step-level ceiling from 1.76% to 15.57%,
  which is the same order as the shell rate rather than far below
  it. Result 1 is therefore sensitive to a judgement call, and no
  amount of arguing about verbs settles it.

  within-session measurement asks whether a ruleset written before

  taxonomy, and no judgement of mine. That is now the load-bearing
  result, and this sensitivity is the strongest argument for why.
